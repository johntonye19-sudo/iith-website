Centre for Vocational & Entrepreneurial Studies

Empowering skills, driving innovation, creating opportunities.

CVES is a dynamic hub for skills acquisition, innovation, and enterprise development. Programmes are designed with strong emphasis on hands-on learning, enabling participants to develop technical proficiency, entrepreneurial insight, and the confidence to create value. Training aligns with the National Skills Qualification Framework (NSQF).
