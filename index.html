Message from the Rector

Welcome to IITH Yenagoa

Welcome to the International Institute of Tourism and Hospitality (IITH), Yenagoa, Bayelsa State — a premier institution located in the heart of the Niger Delta, dedicated to excellence in Tourism, Hospitality, and allied disciplines.

Established in 2012 by the Bayelsa State Government and accredited by NBTE in 2021, we have expanded to seven disciplines at both ND and HND levels, alongside our Centre for Vocational and Entrepreneurial Studies.

Our vision is to become a leading academic institution globally — providing innovative entrepreneurial skills for our graduates to be creative, self-reliant and to render service to humanity.
