Admissions 2026/2027

Applications for National Diploma (ND) and Higher National Diploma (HND) programmes are now open. Apply and begin your journey to a rewarding career.

Available Programmes

What You Can Study

ND

National Diploma Programmes

  • ✓ Tourism Management Technology
  • ✓ Hospitality Management Technology
  • ✓ Accountancy
  • ✓ Business Administration and Management
  • ✓ Public Administration
  • ✓ Computer Science
  • ✓ Fashion Design and Clothing Technology
HND

Higher National Diploma

  • ✓ Tourism Management Technology
  • ✓ Hospitality Management Technology

HND programmes require a relevant National Diploma certificate.

ND Entry Requirements

  • 1. Five (5) credit passes in relevant O'Level subjects including Mathematics and English Language at not more than two sittings (WAEC, NECO or NABTEB).
  • 2. Acceptable JAMB UTME score (typically 100 and above — check current cutoff).
  • 3. Successful completion of the Institute's screening/post-UTME exercise where applicable.

HND Entry Requirements

  • 1. National Diploma (ND) in a relevant field (Tourism Management Technology or Hospitality Management Technology) with a minimum of Lower Credit.
  • 2. Evidence of one-year industrial training (SIWES) where required.
  • 3. Five (5) O'Level credits including Mathematics and English.
