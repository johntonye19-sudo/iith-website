News & Events

Stay updated with the latest happenings at IITH Yenagoa.

MAY 2026

EDTF Boss Makes Surprise Visit to IITH Yenagoa

The Executive Secretary of the Education Development Trust Fund (EDTF), Professor Ebimieowei Etebu, paid an unscheduled visit to the Institute. The Rector and Management conducted a tour of facilities including the bakery and fashion studio. The visit underscores ongoing support for institutional development.

2025

IITH Hosts Maiden National Annual Conference

The Institute successfully hosted its first National Annual Conference, bringing together scholars, practitioners and students. The conference called for implementation of national standards in the tourism and hospitality industry and reviewed the national tourism policy.

ONGOING

2026/2027 Admissions Now Open

Applications for National Diploma and Higher National Diploma programmes are open. Prospective students are invited to apply for programmes across Tourism, Hospitality, Business, Computer Science, Fashion and Public Administration.

PARTNERSHIP

ITF Assesses IITH as SUPA Centre

The Industrial Training Fund (ITF) South-South Director visited the Institute to evaluate capacity and readiness to serve as a Centre for the Skill-Up Artisan Program (SUPA), aligning with national skills development goals.
